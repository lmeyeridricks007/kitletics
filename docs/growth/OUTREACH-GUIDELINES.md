# Outreach guidelines

Drafts are generated in the opportunity page. **Nothing is auto-sent.**

## Voice

- Short. Specific. Useful.
- Name the piece of theirs you actually looked at, or skip the fake compliment.
- Never open with “I love your article”.
- Never ask for “a backlink”. Offer a dataset, tool, or explainer they can cite.
- No fake familiarity. Contact is unknown until a human confirms it.

## Shape

1. Subject: topic + what they can cite
2. Opening: why this page of theirs
3. Why relevant: one sentence
4. Asset: live URL only
5. Evidence: what the dataset covers / sample caveats
6. CTA: “if useful for a future update…”

## Example

> I saw your guide to heel-to-toe drop. We keep a searchable dataset of current running shoes with weight, drop and stack. If it is useful when you next update the piece, it is here: `/running/shoes/database`.

## Follow-up

- Wait 5–7 days.
- One follow-up is default; two is the ceiling.
- Stop immediately on decline, link earned, or mention earned.
- Do not chase club/federation inboxes weekly.

## Forbidden

Comment spam, directory blasts, fake journalist names, purchased guest posts, reciprocal schemes, sitewide footer asks, inventing an editor@ address.

**Forums / Reddit:** never auto-post. Draft a helpful answer first. Include a Kitletics URL only when the page materially helps and community rules are known to allow it. If rules are unknown, mark `RULES_UNKNOWN` and do not use `LINK_RECOMMENDED`. See `GROKBOT-GROWTH-OUTREACH-AGENT.md`.

If you cannot confirm a contact, leave `CONTACT_UNKNOWN` and find a public masthead or source-request thread. Store the provenance URL in notes.
