# Backlink strategy

Optimize for **relevant editorial mentions**, not referring-domain count.

## What we want

A journalist, coach, club, or researcher citing a Kitletics page because it is the most useful current source — usually the Running Shoe Database, the Finder, or a methodology-backed guide.

## What we do not want

- High-DA sites with no running/fitness editorial fit
- Sitewide footer / blogroll / profile links
- Paid guest posts, link insertions, PBNs
- Homepage links as the default ask
- Invented emails or invented DR/DA

## Priority order for pages we promote

1. Running Shoe Database (`/running/shoes/database`)
2. Running Shoe Finder (`/tools/running-shoe-finder`)
3. Future market research (no URL until it ships)
4. Major Best Guides (only for roundups / shopping desks)
5. Buying guides (clubs, coaches, explainers)
6. Named comparisons (brand vs brand stories)
7. Strong individual reviews (only when the story is that model)
8. Brand / category hubs only when the story is the lineup

Never lead with `/`.
