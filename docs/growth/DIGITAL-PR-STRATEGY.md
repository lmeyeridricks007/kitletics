# Digital PR strategy

Digital PR here means **data-led stories from the canonical Running Shoe Database**, not a newsroom stunt calendar.

## Rules

- Findings must come from `computeRunningShoeMarketInsights` (or a later dated snapshot). If it is not computable, the idea stays `needs_data` / `parked`.
- Do not pitch unpublished Market 2026 numbers.
- Sample size and exclusions travel with every claim.
- Prices are regional From-prices, never invented MSRP.

## Story types that can ship from the live catalog

| Idea | When it is pitchable |
| --- | --- |
| Lightest daily trainers by brand | When the weight insight has rows |
| Highest-stack cohort | When quality-valid stack exists |
| Lowest-drop trainers | When drop insight has rows |
| Average From-price by brand (NL) | When verified offers cover a brand sample |
| Carbon vs non-carbon | When plated vs non-plated cohorts compute |

Parked until we have dated snapshots: “trends over time”.

## Desks to approach (after a finding exists)

- Running gear editors (ProRun, iRunFar, Road Trail Run)
- Independent newsletters (The Morning Shakeout)
- Consumer fitness/commerce (Men's Health, Outside) — only with a tight finding
- Sports-science explainers (The Conversation) — methodology + database, never a Best-of page

## Source-request platforms

HARO, Qwoted, Featured, SourceBottle, Source of Sources — **manual capture only**. Paste into Imports. Deadlines show on Digital PR. Do not scrape gated inboxes.

## Campaign 3 (Market 2026)

Status `planned`. Do not email WIRED or Runner's World with a teaser that implies the report is live. Offer the live database today; offer the report when a URL exists.
