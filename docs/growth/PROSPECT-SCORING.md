# Prospect scoring

Overall = weighted clamp 0–100.

| Dimension | Weight | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relevance | 30% | Running / fitness / the specific topic |
| Authority | 20% | Editorial judgment, **not DA/DR** |
| Asset fit | 20% | Do we have the right live page? |
| Likelihood | 15% | Will they actually cite a third-party resource? |
| Editorial quality | 10% | Real articles vs marketplace / thin SEO |
| Relationship | 5% | Existing warmth (usually 0–20 on seed) |

Bands: **90–100 MUST PURSUE** · **75–89 HIGH** · **60–74 MEDIUM** · **&lt;60 LOW**.

A relevant Dutch running title with moderate authority can beat WIRED on a weak shoe story. That is intentional.

Imported Ahrefs Domain Rating / Semrush Authority Score / Moz DA / Majestic CF are labeled on the row and **must not replace** `authorityScore`.

## Risk

| Band | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| SAFE | No spam/PBN/marketplace pattern in the text we have |
| REVIEW | Paid, sitewide, retailer, or ambiguous commercial |
| AVOID | Casino/adult/crypto, guest-post farms, PBN-like, write-for-us mills |

AVOID rows are not outreach targets even if the score is high.

## Review

No auto-approve. Human must see: why this site, why this asset, why this angle, evidence, risk.
