Product description

ADV Essence Wind Tights are highly functional training tights with windproof front and a soft brushed inside for intense workouts in cold and windy conditions. These advanced training tights are made of a soft and functional jersey fabric with brushed inside for extra warmth and feature a windproof front that protects against cold wind. The tights also come with drawcord at waist for optimal fit and back pocket with zipper for safe storage of keys. Eco-friendly fabric ADV Essence Wind Tights are made from recycled polyester and are part of our ambition to increase the proportion of sustainable materials in our products and achieve the goal of taking full responsibility for the entire value chain. The recycled polyester is not only eco-friendly but offers exceptional moisture transport and ventilation as well. • Soft, elastic and functional jersey fabric made from recycled polyester and elastane • Brushed inside for extra warmth and comfort • Windproof front • Waistband with adjustable inner drawcord • Zip pocket at back yoke • Reflective prints
Article number: 1909606-396000
