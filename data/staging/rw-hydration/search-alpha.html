Overview

Store all your hydration and key essentials in comfort with the UltrAspire Alpha 6.0 Vest. This vest features UltrAcool Light Mesh for faster sweat-wicking and cooler on-body temperatures. Also, the updated magnetic closure on the back allows for the securing of larger items with easy access while on the run. Double layer 30D fabric is added to key stress points on the vest increasing durability and key loops at create additional storage points.

This pack's ErgoFit design uses a blend of angled straps and weight-balancing that naturally adapts to your body's movement while reducing fatigue. A water-resistant, magnet-sealed pocket on the left shoulder strap can safely store hydration pills and tablets, while multiple pockets on the front and side can accommodate your favorite hydration bottles and gear, such as gloves or a headlamp. Nutrition and extra layers fit easily within the rear compartment and can be pulled out on the go through the magnet sealed side openings.
