100% trail running since 1999

As a French brand, pioneer in Trail Running, based in Chartreuse, Raidlight is committed to designing and manufacturing eco-responsible technical, lightweight, and high-performance equipment to outfit all trail runners from head to toe. Since 1999, by sharing our expertise and commitment with a community of enthusiasts, we believe in the development of French-style Trailrunning—that is, a French brand that is committed, bold, dynamic, and proud of its products!
