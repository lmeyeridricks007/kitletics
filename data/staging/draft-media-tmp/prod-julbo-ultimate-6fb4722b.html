    Color: Dark Gray / Army Green With REACTIV 0-3 High Contrast Lens

    Designed for running and cycling, the Ultimate features ultra-light frames, panoramic REACTIV or Spectron lenses, and CustomFIT temples for a personalized, secure fit.

    Ideal For: MTB, Running, Cycling , Nighttime Use
    LENS DETAIL
    DESCRIPTION

    Push every aspect of your performance with Ultimate. Each line and detail of this models geometry is designed around one goal only: ultimate freedom in sport. CustomFIT Temples with Grip Tech structure and a 3D Nose offer adjustability for a personalized and secure fit. The wide, panoramic lens gives a modern look, providing protection, a massive field of vision, and visual comfort. Ultimate will help you push your limits.
    PRODUCT FEATURES
    • 3D Nose Fit: Adjustable nose pads fit any nose shape and ensure all-day comfort and security.  (Compatible with Optical Clip Prescription Insert)
    • Comfort Fit with Helmet: Shape, size and features are designed to fit comfortably with any helmet.
    • Cord Attachment: Temples are shaped to work perfectly with a Julbo cord, keeping sunglasses secure in all conditions.
    • CustomFIT Temples: Precise temple adjustability guarantees a perfect fit and secure grip.
    • Full Venting: Multiple large vents in the frame allow for air circulation to prevent fogging.
    • Grip Tech Temples: Soft, rubberized temples offer comfort and perfect grip.
    • Panoramic View: Oversized lens offers a large and unobstructed field of view.
    • 100% UV Protection: Julbo lenses block 100% of UV a, b, & c rays.
    Color: Dark Gray / Army Green With REACTIV 0-3 High Contrast Lens

      Beautiful glasses but the tech..

      First of all I'd like to say Julbo customer service has been among the best I've had lately with a gear company. So it pains me to leave a less than stellar review.

      In the process of replacing some old Zebra lens Julbo glasses I loved, it seems like Julbo has had some regression of lens changing tech or something. I've found with the photochromatic lenses, if a wear a hat, they don't darken fully. To me it seems a bit absurd as in strong sun wearing a hat seems pretty normal, but then the glasses don't get as dark as I'd like. I notice this if I've had them off and in the sun or resting on my hat and bring them down, they get lighter.

      The old zebra lenses had a great mirror finish so even when they were at their least dim, people couldn't see your eyes. These new ones don't quite have the mirror to do so if they're dim.
