USED BY ELITES

A quick liquid carbohydrate gel that’s best used during intense exercise sessions to maintain energy and delay exhaustion. The isotonic formula absorbs quickly, so you can fuel without bloating or needing extra fluids.

Recognized as the world’s first isotonic energy gel and winner of the Queen’s Award for Enterprise (Innovation) in 2015, it remains the go-to choice for top athletes across endurance and team sports worldwide.

Why Elite Athletes Choose GO Isotonic Gels

  • Supports steady energy to help you maintain pace longer
  • Unique formula means no need for extra fluid intake
  • Absorbs smoothly to prevent stomach upset
  • No artificial flavors, just clean, effective ingredients

SUGGESTED USE

Depending on your sport and intensity, aim for 1–3 gels per hour to reach a 60–90g carbohydrate target.

Tear open, squeeze, and go whenever you need a quick energy boost

ENGINEERED FOR ENERGY

Depleting carbohydrate stores is one of the biggest causes of fatigue during exercise. Replacing those stores with fast-acting carbohydrates improves endurance, delays fatigue, and keeps performance levels high when the effort intensifies.

FUEL DURING EXERCISE

To maintain optimal energy, consume 60-90g of carbohydrates per hour during training or competition. The GO Isotonic Energy Gel provides rapid energy uptake without adding liquid volume, so you stay fueled and focused, not heavy or sluggish.

SUPPORT THE SWEAT

Each gel helps stabilize blood glucose, sustain endurance, and prevent “hitting the wall.” By delivering quick, efficient carbohydrate replenishment, it supports your body’s glycogen stores and keeps muscles performing longer. Designed for endurance athletes and team players alike, this gel works as hard as you do, especially when fatigue sets in.

WHAT IS ISOTONIC?

“Isotonic” means the gel matches your body’s natural fluid balance, allowing it to empty from the stomach quickly and deliver energy immediately: no extra water needed. This is also known as osmosis, and it helps maintain the balance of blood cells by maintaining the same concentration of solutes as the fluid inside the cells. This allows your cells to function properly.

WHAT IS ISOTONIC?

Created in 2000, the GO Isotonic Energy Gel provides a convenient source of carbohydrates and hydration to support your performance. Its isotonic formula is designed to match the body’s natural fluid balance, allowing for rapid absorption and easier digestion during exercise. By helping maintain hydration and energy levels, this gel can help you sustain your effort for longer and reduce the risk of fatigue-related issues.
