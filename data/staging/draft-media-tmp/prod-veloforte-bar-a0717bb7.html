Focus Is A Recipe

We’ve always been told performance is a ‘formula’ — an equation of macronutrients — without any consideration for what the human body actually needs or can use. Instead, Ciocco’s all-natural ingredients combine to boost concentration and motivation, fuelling brain as much as body.

Rich in flavanol antioxidants, cocoa helps support circulation and alertness, giving you a real mental lift. All cooked up with an optimal carbs-to-protein ratio, with 35g steady-release glucose & fructose that fuel you to go the distance, and 4.7g plant protein that helps you grow ready for the next one.

When it all comes from real ingredients, you don’t have to compromise on health, performance or flavour. You just get a bar that lives up to the same uncompromising standards you set for yourself.

Because focus is a recipe, and nature had it all along.

Optimally balanced for performance

Our 8:1 carb-to-protein ratio works to fuel you towards the finish line and begin your recovery for the next start line.

Great Tasting. Just ask our awards.

A 47-time Great Taste Award winner, we’re proud of everything we cook up, including this winning bar.

Baked not faked

Not a synthetic or artificial ingredient in the kitchen, our natural energy bars are made with real ingredients, giving you health benefits beyond macros.

Enjoy Ciocco before, during and after exercise.

• Before: Eat a Ciocco bar 1-2 hours before a training session

• During: Fuel long workouts and efforts by taking on 45-60g carbs per hour (more for intense efforts). Each Ciocco bar delivers 35g natural carbs per bar plus 5g of protein

• After: Enjoy a bar within 30 minutes of intense or prolonged exercise to restore healthy muscle function*

Dates (35%), almonds (23%), cane sugar, honey, cocoa powder (7%), agave syrup, tapioca flour, mixed spices, wafer paper (potato starch), vanilla extract, vanilla seeds. Allergens: See ingredients in bold 

Not suitable for people with nut or peanut allergies.

We use whole fruits and whole nuts in our products, whilst every effort is made to remove all fruit stones & nut shells, there may be the occasional fragment. They last 12 months when stored in a cool dry place.

They sit within our Perform range, made for active people who want real nutrition without the trade-offs.
