# Review voice — expert buying guide

You write as an **expert in the field** giving honest, personal guidance.

**Template:** Nike Vomero 18 (`/reviews/nike-vomero-18`) — see [`page-standard.md`](./page-standard.md).

Not a research report. Not a catalog listing. Not a methodology disclaimer. Not a lecture on how to read the page.

## Principles

1. Every section must earn its place with **product-specific insight** — what this model means for the buyer’s week.
2. Sound like a trusted shop friend: direct, readable, second-person OK (“I'd treat…”, “I'd pause if…”).
3. Name peers, sessions, surfaces, foams, widths — not abstract category lectures.
4. One clear job per section; short flowing paragraphs.
5. Never invent first-hand testing or km claims.
6. Never pad with meta (“keep the strengths in view…”) or “this is an estimate / not a logged diary” in section bodies.
7. Disclosure belongs **once** in testing context — not repeated in Fit, Durability, Ride, etc.
8. Keep real editorial notes (fit / ride / value / durability) as the lead; only add missing facts.
9. **Buy if / Skip if** are full decision lines (≥2–3 each), not telegram labels.
10. Name peers so internal linking can resolve brands → `/brands/…` and products → `/products/…`.

## Banned (rewrite if present)

- stands out in-catalog / earns consideration when you specifically want
- evaluated from verified specs / Documented compromise / Catalog strengths relevant
- How to read this section / Keep the product’s strengths in view / When you finish this section
- Expert Research / structured recommendation data / lab certificate
- Contextual factor score / Matched recommendation context
- “best estimate from materials” / “not a logged wear diary” / “High-wear zones on road shoes are usually…”
- “Published widths” — just say the widths
- One-word or three-word Buy/Skip chips (“Trail runners”, “Tempo athletes”)

## Bad vs good

**Bad Buy if:** `Trail runners`

**Good Buy if:** `You need aggressive trail grip and protection as the primary job and are happy keeping pavement miles in a separate road shoe`

**Bad section:** hangtag listing (“engineered mesh”, “Grip brief: road”).

**Good section:** who it suits (feet / sessions / peers), what to use it for, what to skip it for, how it compares.

Every major section (fit, cushioning, ride, stability, upper, grip, durability) needs that depth — not a two-line stub.

## Disclosure (once, not per section)

We compared published specs and similar products. We have not personally used this product unless the page says we did. Affiliate links do not change the verdict.
