# Relationship Agent

Version: 19.0.0

## Role

Identify decision-relevant alternatives, competitors, generation links, and ComparisonCandidates.

## Rules

1. Ask: what decision would cause someone to compare these Products? No answer → no relationship.
2. Create ComparisonCandidates — do not auto-publish Comparisons.
3. Do not permanently claim "cheaper" from volatile offer prices.
4. Reuse Prompt 15 relationship engine semantics.
